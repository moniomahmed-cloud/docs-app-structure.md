# Aura & Glow Wellbeing App — Design System

## Brand Style

Aura & Glow should feel:

- Elegant
- Calm
- Modern
- Premium
- Warm
- Clean
- Wellness-focused

## Primary Brand Direction

The visual identity should combine beauty, skincare, and wellbeing.

The design should use generous spacing, soft shapes, elegant typography, and high-quality imagery.

## UI Style

- Rounded cards
- Soft buttons
- Clean layouts
- Large product photography
- Simple navigation
- Clear call-to-action buttons
- Minimal visual clutter

## Main Screens

- Splash Screen
- Home
- Shop
- Product Details
- Wellbeing
- Open Your Salon
- Cart
- Checkout
- Orders
- Account

## Design Principle

Every screen should be simple enough for a first-time user to understand immediately.

The customer should always know:

1. Where they are
2. What they can do
3. What to tap next
